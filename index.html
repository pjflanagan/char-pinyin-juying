Mandarin Study Resources

* These were built by a student, not a native speaker. There may be errors. Characters default to the most common pronunciation which may not be correct in all contexts.

  • Reading Tool - Type or copy/paste text to see pinyin and zhuyin, reformats to traditional.
  • Basic Flashcards - These flashcards are some of the most common characters, typically taught in beginner lessons.
  • Reddit Flashcards - Flashcards sourced from a Reddit post (I do not remember the source).
  • Curated Flashcards - Coming soon, categorized flashcards that I curated myself.
  • Zhuyin Flashcards - Randomly pick a character and display only the zhuyin to practice reading.

Other Resources

  • Songs - A collection of popular karaoke songs in mandarin with pinyin/english subtitles.
