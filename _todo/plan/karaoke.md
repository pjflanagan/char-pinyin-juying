
# Karaoke

There is a songs folder in data.
There will be matching files, one csv of the lyrics, and one json of the song metadata.

I want to make a page similar to flashcards. We select a song we want to study in a modal
Then we are brought to the page where we are shown a spotify widget to play the song as well as the lyrics.

You can toggle the pinyin visibility.
You can hover to reveal English.
